Objektif

Objective

  1. Menjelaskan salah faham tentang Islam melalui dakwah dan kebajikan.
  2. Pendidikan dan latihan kepada murid madrasah dan memberi kemahiran kepada orang dewasa, mengikut minat masing masing.
  3. Membantu usahawan-usahawan mudah kearah membuka dan melakukan peniagaan yang halal.
  4. Mewujudkan amal jariah antara yang berharta dengan yang memerlukan.
  5. Menyediakan pusat jagaan anak yatim dan warga emas di setiap negeri Malaysia dan luar negara.

Objective

Objektif

  1. To clear misconceptions about Islam through da’wah and welfare.
  2. To provide education and training to madrasah students and equip adults with skills based on their interests.
  3. To assist young entrepreneurs in establishing and conducting halal businesses.
  4. To create ongoing charity between those who have wealth and those in need.
  5. To establish care centers for orphans and senior citizens in every state of Malaysia and abroad.
