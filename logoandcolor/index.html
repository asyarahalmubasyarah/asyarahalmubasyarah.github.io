Logo dan Warna

Logo and Color

REKA BENTUK LOGO Logo Pertubuhan ini mengandungi bentuk-bentuk atau lambang tertentu. Antara bentuk yang ada ialah kubah, bulan sabit dan Rub al-hizb. Kubah bermaksud Pertubuhan Al Mubasyarah menjadi pusat pendidikan berteraskan Al-Quran sekaligus menjadi rumah kepada pelajar tahfiz. Bulan sabit membawa maksud Islam sebagai agama rasmi dan anak bulan bermaksud pelajar tahfiz ini bakal menjadi agen perubahan di dalam negara. Rub al-hizb ialah sebuah lambang Islam, yang digariskan sebagai dua segi empat sama yang bertindih. Angka 10 dalam bahasa Arab yang terletak di antara perkataan Pertubuhan Al Mubasyarah memberi maksud 10 orang sahabat nabi yang terpilih dijanjikan khabar gembira menjadi penghuni syurga.

WARNA LOGO Warna hijau melambangkan kesucian Agama Islam. Warna kuning gelap melambangkan kebesaran Sultan yang berdaulat sebagai Yang Di Pertuan Agong dan Penaung Rakyat serta menjadi Ketua Hal Ehwal Agama. Warna kuning cair memberi maksud anak tahfiz ini bakal menjadi cahaya yang gemerlapan sebagai pewaris negara yang hafaz Al-Quran, cekap berdakwah dan memiliki kemahiran dalam bidang profesional.

Logo and Color

Logo dan Warna

THE DESIGN OF THE LOGO The logo of the organization contains various symbols that represent its values and mission. The dome represents the organization as a center for Quranic education and a home for Tahfiz students. The crescent moon symbolizes Islam as the official religion, while the crescent shape itself represents the students as agents of change in the country. The Rub al-hizb is an Islamic symbol that consists of two overlapping squares, and the number 10 in Arabic represents the ten companions of the Prophet who were promised paradise. Overall, the logo appears to reflect the organization's commitment to Islamic education and its role in nurturing students to become leaders and positive contributors to society.

THE COLORS OF THE LOGO The green color represents the purity of Islam, while the dark yellow color symbolizes the sovereignty of the Sultan who is the Yang Di Pertuan Agong and the Patron of the people, as well as the Head of Religious Affairs. The light yellow color signifies that the Tahfiz students will become shining beacons as the inheritors of the country, who memorize the Quran, possess professional skills, and are capable of preaching effectively. Overall, the colors used in the logo seem to represent the values and aspirations of the organization and its mission to produce well-rounded and competent Islamic scholars.
